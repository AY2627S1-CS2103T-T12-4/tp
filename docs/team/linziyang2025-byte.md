### Project: TAssist

TAssist is a desktop app for CS2040S teaching assistants to track tutorial attendance, class participation, and
assignment submissions.

Given below are my contributions to the project.

* **Code contributed**: [RepoSense link](https://nus-cs2103-ay2627-s1.github.io/tp-dashboard/?search=linziyang2025-byte&breakdown=true)

* **Features**:
  * The TAssist model of groups and students, and showing the active group in the GUI.
  * `group export` to save a group's records as a CSV file.
  * `attendance clear` and `participation clear` to remove a wrong record.
  * Names with `s/o`, `d/o`, `a/l` or `a/p` in `student add`.
  * The in-app help for every command, and the app icon.

* **Documentation**: the UG sections for these features, and the use cases, NFRs and related DG sections and
  diagrams.

* **Team tasks**: the end-to-end acceptance pass, alpha testing (5 bugs reported), and reviewing teammates' PRs.
